## 📂 Portfolio Projects

### 1. [NYC 311 — Damaged Tree Complaints](https://github.com/kyawkyawhtun-nyc/nyc-311-analysis)
Which ZIP codes wait longest for tree-damage service — and are the worst hotspots the same every year?
- Analyzed 3.4M+ service requests (2024) via the NYC Open Data API, and 2021 records in BigQuery SQL
- **Finding:** the worst ZIP in 3 of 5 boroughs was identical in 2021 and 2024 — tree-damage hotspots are *sticky*
- **Finding:** Staten Island waits longest overall (18.5 days average); "Tree Alive – in Poor Condition" waits ~130 days, while fallen trees are cleared in ~36 — speed follows damage already done, not damage about to happen

### 2. [NYC Yellow Taxi — When and Where Does the City Move?](https://github.com/kyawkyawhtun-nyc/nyc-taxi-analysis)
Cleaned 36.3M trip records to 33.67M valid trips in BigQuery SQL (five documented validation rules) and analyzed demand by month, hour, place, and payment type.
- **Finding:** peak hour is 6 PM (2.40M trips) — the city taxis home more than to work; busiest month is October
- **Finding:** December 2022 is missing from the public table itself (57 rows) — the analysis is scoped honestly to Jan–Nov 2022
- **Finding:** cash trips record $0 tips because cash tips never enter the data — invisible, not zero
- **Finding:** #1 pickup zone is JFK Airport (1.65M); the other nine of the top 10 are all in Manhattan
- Looker Studio dashboard in progress (KPI row and monthly chart verified against the SQL)
